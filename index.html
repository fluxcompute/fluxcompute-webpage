How it works

01

you define the limits

Set the budget, latency, and quality bounds for the whole run, not for each call.

02

Before each step, fluxcompute looks at what's left of your budget, latency, and quality targets and chooses how to run it.

03

finish inside your limits

Every run ends within the bounds you set, with a full record of each decision and why it was made.
